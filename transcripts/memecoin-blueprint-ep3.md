# Memecoin Blueprint - Episode 3 Transcript

Date: 2026-08-27
Source: YouTube transcript provided by user
Topic: Scanning, narratives, wallet tracking, and coin-type selection

## Key Insights
- Wallet tracking for alpha, not copy trading: track top wallets to see narratives and who is in your coins.
- Red flag: 2-3 tracked wallets in a coin under 10k market cap.
- Coin types to trade:
  - Community coins: preferred, community-driven.
  - Tweet coins with attached community/thesis: acceptable.
- Coin types to avoid:
  - News coins: often vamped, pure speculation.
  - Vamp coins: relaunched runners, bundled to 5-10k then dumped.
  - Profile coins: always avoid.
- Narrative checks for community coins:
  - Viewers vs holders ratio: low watcher ratio suggests multi-wallet bundling.
  - Ticker bond history: if same ticker bonded multiple times, avoid.
  - Community health:
    - CA in description
    - Pinned thesis post
    - Active moderation (no bot/spam posts)
  - Dex paid banner check: borders on Dexscreener banner suggest third-party launch tools/bundling.
  - Recent post engagement: 100+ views and 10+ likes within 5-10 minutes indicates activity.
- Tweet coin rule: only trade tweets with pre-made community and thesis.

- Added wallet-tracking guidance to research workflow.
- Added coin-type filters and narrative checks to decision rubric.
- Added community activity metrics to holder/social checks.
